Stage 1: Acoustic Perception

Deep Acoustic Emission Feature Extraction

Coupling deep semantic anomaly classification with raw physical acoustic RMS energy to detect incipient dielectric faults before thermal sensors register heat.

🎛️

Log-Mel Filterbank Audio Transform

Continuous acoustic hydrophone signals are transformed into 128-band log-Mel spectrograms (n_mels=128, window length=2048, hop length=512) over 3.0-second sliding windows. Captures high-frequency acoustic shockwaves (10 kHz – 150 kHz) induced by partial discharge arcing.

🧠

SoundGridCNN Classifier

4-stage convolutional backbone with Batch Normalization and Dropout: Conv2D(32) → Conv2D(64) → Conv2D(128) → Conv2D(256) → Linear(1). Achieves 85.08% validation accuracy, 0.9258 ROC-AUC, and an F1-score of 0.85 on verified acoustic failure benchmarks.

⚡

Orthogonal RMS Energy Channel

Calculates normalized acoustic Root Mean Square energy: E_rms = sqrt((1/N) Σ x[n]²). Prevents false trips from low-energy acoustic ambient noise while disambiguating violent electrical flashovers from mild magnetostriction hum.
