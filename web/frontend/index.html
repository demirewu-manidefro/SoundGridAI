Cyber-Physical System Architecture

Mathematical underpinnings of the acoustic perception pipeline and derivative-augmented Markov decision process.

📐

8D Derivative State Space

Extends the state vector with temporal rates of change: [P, ΔP, E, L, ΔL, T/100, ΔT/10, t/t_max]. Resolves the Partial Observability Markov Decision Process (POMDP) by exposing thermal inertia and acoustic arcing acceleration.

⚖️

Anti-Gaming Reward Function

Formulated as R = 40.0 × (L - 0.5) - P_thermal - P_penalty. Penalizes cycling and enforces severe quadratic costs on overheating (>95°C) and arcing damage, making emergency trip the strictly dominant action during dielectric failure.

SoundGridCNN Acoustic Perception

Processes 128-band log-Mel spectrograms extracted from piezoelectric hydrophones. Detects transient high-frequency dielectric breakdown shockwaves in oil hundreds of steps before core heat reaches trip thresholds.
