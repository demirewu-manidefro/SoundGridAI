Engineering Retrospective

Encountered Challenges & Technical Solutions

በምርምሩ ወቅት ያጋጠሙን ወሳኝ ችግሮች እና እንዴት እንደፈታናቸው የተዘጋጀ ሳይንሳዊ ትንተና (A rigorous breakdown of key stumbling blocks and our mathematical/architectural fixes).

❌ Challenge 1: POMDP Thermal Inertia Blindness

Static States Caused Policy Dithering & Oscillation

በመጀመሪያው ዙር static የሙቀት ($T$) እና የአኖማሊ ($P$) እሴቶችን ብቻ ስንጠቀም፣ ኤጀንቱ ትራንስፎርመሩ በፍጥነት እየጋለ መሆኑን ($\dot{T} > 0$) ወይስ እየቀዘቀዘ መሆኑን ($\dot{T} < 0$) ማወቅ አልቻለም ነበር። ይህም የውሳኔ መደናገርን (Policy Dithering) እና ያለ በቂ ምክንያት መብራት ማጥፋትን አስከትሎ ነበር።

✅ Mathematical Solution: 8D Rate-of-Change

ከባድና የዘገዩ RNN/LSTM ኔትወርኮችን ከመጠቀም ይልቅ፣ የ 8D State Space በመፍጠር 3 የመጀመሪያ-ደረጃ ጊዜያዊ ለውጦችን (First-order derivatives: ΔP, ΔL, ΔT) ጨመርን። ይህም POMDPን ወደ ሙሉ Markovian ውሳኔ ቀይሮ የዘገየ ሙቀትን አስቀድሞ እንዲያውቅ አደረገው።

❌ Challenge 2: Reward Gaming & Load Churning

The Agent Learned to Exploit Instant Transition Bonuses

ኤጀንቱ የረጅም ጊዜ የትራንስፎርመር ሙቀትን ችላ በማለት፣ ሎዱን በየሰከንዱ ከ 0% ወደ 100% በመቀያየር (Load Cycling) አጫጭር የሪዋርድ ነጥቦችን ለመሰብሰብ ሞክሮ ነበር (Reward Gaming)። ይህ በእውነተኛ የኤሌክትሪክ ግሪድ ላይ ከባድ የሃይል መዋዠቅ ያስከትላል።

✅ Physics Solution: Quadratic Joule Heating Penalty

የጁል ሙቀት ቀመርን ($q_{\text{gen}} = 4.2 L^2 + 10 P$) በቀጥታ በሪዋርዱ ውስጥ አካተን አላስፈላጊ የሎድ መቀያየርን በከፍተኛ ቅጣት (ΔL penalty) ቀጣነው። ይህም ኤጀንቱ የተረጋጋ እና ቀጣይነት ያለው የሃይል ማስተላለፍ እንዲመርጥ አደረገው።

❌ Challenge 3: Arcing Flashover vs Ambient Noise

Acoustic Noise False Alarms in Substation Environments

በማከፋፈያ ጣቢያዎች (Substations) ውስጥ ባሉ ሌሎች ድምጾች ምክንያት፣ የድምጽ ክላሲፋየሩ ቀላል ጫጫታዎችን እንደ ከፍተኛ የአደጋ ብልጭታ (Arcing) በመቁጠር የውሸት መብራት የማጥፋት ስጋት ፈጥሮ ነበር።

✅ Dual-Perception Solution: Semantic + Physical RMS

የ CNN Semantic probability ($P_{\text{anom}}$) ብቻ ከመጠቀም ይልቅ፣ እውነተኛ የድምጽ ሃይል ጥንካሬን (Physical Acoustic RMS Energy $E_t$) እንደ ሁለተኛ ቻናል አገናኘን። ይህም ቀላል ድምጾችን ከትክክለኛ ከፍተኛ የ dielectric arcing ፍንዳታዎች በግልጽ ለይቶ አወጣ።

❌ Challenge 4: Policy Mode Collapse under Incipient PD

Developing PD Caused 98.3% Throttle Over-Conservatism

የ 100-Episode Monte Carlo ሙከራው እንዳሳየው፣ ገና በማቆጥቆጥ ላይ ባለ የብልሽት ድምጽ ወቅት ኤጀንቱ ከመጠን በላይ በመፍራት 98.3% ጊዜ ሎዱን ወደ 10% ዝቅ አደረገው (Mode Collapse to Action 2)። ይህም ለግሪድ አቅርቦት እጥረት ይዳርጋል።

✅ Production Solution: Temperature Scaling & Baseline Incentive

የ CNN log-odds እሴቶችን በ Temperature Scaling በማስተካከል የጥንቃቄ ድንበሩን አረምን። በተጨማሪም በመደበኛ ሁኔታ ወቅት Maintain Loadን የሚያበረታታ አወንታዊ ሪዋርድ በማስገባት ከመጠን ያለፈ የሎድ ቅነሳን አስቀረን።

Safe SoundGrid Cyber-Physical Monitoring

Acoustic PD Detection within 1 Step

SoundGrid AI continuously decodes oil hydrophone vibrations. The moment partial discharge exceeds threshold (P ≥ 0.70), it safely opens the circuit breaker in a single step (100% trip rate in severe arcing), preserving transformer integrity.

Catastrophic Burnout Under Blind Relays

Conventional Relays (IEC 60255) Are Acoustically Blind

Standard industrial relays measure only oil heat and overcurrent. When violent arcing discharges occur, core temperatures require tens of minutes to heat external oil reservoirs, resulting in explosive transformer tank ruptures and grid fires.

Scientific Architecture

Acoustic Perception & Cyber-Physical Pipeline

Deep acoustic feature engineering coupled with the derivative-augmented Markov decision process.

Stage 1: SoundGridCNN Feature Extractor (85.08% Accuracy | 0.9258 ROC-AUC)

🎛️

Log-Mel Filterbank Audio Transform

Continuous hydrophone signals are transformed into 128-band log-Mel spectrograms (n_mels=128, window=2048, hop=512) over 3.0-second sliding windows to isolate transient discharge spikes.

🧠

4-Stage Convolutional Backbone

Conv2D(32) → Conv2D(64) → Conv2D(128) → Conv2D(256) → Linear(1) with Batch Normalization and Dropout. Validated on authentic acoustic partial discharge benchmarks.

⚡

Orthogonal RMS Energy Channel

Computes physical acoustic RMS energy E_rms. Disambiguates low-energy benign environmental vibrations from violent high-energy internal electrical arcs.

Stage 2: 8-Dimensional Markovian State Formulation (POMDP Resolution)

S = [ s₀: P_anom, s₁: ΔP_anom, s₂: E_acoustic, s₃: Load, s₄: ΔLoad, s₅: T_core/100, s₆: ΔT_core/10, s₇: t/t_max ] ∈ ℜ⁸

By including temporal derivatives (ΔP, ΔL, ΔT), the state transitions become strictly Markovian: the agent knows whether temperature is actively ramping up or safely plateauing, eliminating the need for computationally prohibitive recurrent neural networks (LSTM/GRU) on substation microcontrollers.
